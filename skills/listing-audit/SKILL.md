# Listing audit

## Checks
1. **Cover photo at phone size**: is the main reason to book readable when the image is small and cropped? Rank 3-4 candidates.
2. **First five photos**: do they cover the hero feature, sleeping, living space and the setting, in that order? Propose an order by photo number.
3. **Captions and alt text**: short captions, plus objective descriptions for screen readers.
4. **Mismatches**: conflicts between photos, amenities, description and house rules (e.g. a desk in photos but no "workspace" amenity).
5. **Competitors**: compare price, rating, reviews and features with up to five comparable listings for the same dates (data from the comparables tool). Say where this listing wins and loses.

## Output
A findings list ordered by impact, each with the fix and the effort (minutes, money). Text fixes become `listing-copy` drafts; physical fixes become tasks.
You cannot see the live listing unless photos and text are provided: say so instead of guessing.
