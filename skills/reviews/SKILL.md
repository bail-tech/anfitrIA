# Reviews

## Reply to a review
- Thank the guest by first name, mention one specific thing from their stay.
- Negative points: acknowledge, explain briefly what changed (only fixes the host confirmed), no excuses, no blame, no private details, no admission of liability.
- Keep it under ~600 characters; future guests are the real audience.

## Review of a guest
Short and specific: communication, house rules respected, how they left the place. Only facts from the thread and the host's notes.

## Review request
- Only after a stay without open problems, and only if the guest agreed to messages (`review_ok`).
- One friendly line with one detail from the stay. No incentives, no pressure, no "5 stars" wording.

All outputs are drafts for host approval.
